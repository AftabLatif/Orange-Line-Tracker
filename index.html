📍 Location is off — turn it on for Live GPS

Please note: your browser only shows its own Allow / Deny prompt once. After a denial, this app cannot make that prompt appear again — first switch location on in your browser settings (steps below), then tap Try again. Everything else in the app keeps working without location.

  1. Tap the lock / tune icon in the address bar at the top of your browser.
  2. Tap Site settings → Location → Allow.
  3. Come back here and tap Try again below.

On iPhone Safari: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Safari Websites → While Using the App. Then return and tap Try again.

What about my own live GPS?

That part can be live today — and it is. Tap “Use my live location”, allow location access, and the page plots your phone's real GPS position on the line: a blue YOU marker on the diagram and radar, with your nearest station, distance to the next station and your speed, updating as you move. It is only your position, though — other trains still show timetable estimates because this project has no authorized train-location feed connected. Your location is processed in your browser for the on-screen marker; it is not sent to this project's server.

Why not just show real live GPS?

This project is not connected to an authorized train-location feed. Until a reliable and permitted source is integrated, train positions and arrivals remain timetable-based estimates. We will label any future verified live data separately.

Why do all trains run daily on the same pattern?

The Orange Line is a metro on a fixed, grade-separated track. There is no mixed traffic, no signals shared with cars, and departures are scheduled from both terminals all day. That regularity is exactly why a timetable model works well here — far better than it ever could for a road bus stuck in traffic.

What happens outside service hours?

No trains are scheduled, so the tracker shows a “no trains running” state with the first-train times (6:13 AM from Ali Town, 6:14 AM from Dera Gujran) or, after the last departure, a last-train message. Trains already on their final run are shown until they complete their ~49-minute journey.
